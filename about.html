ABOUT COURSE GUIDE

Empowering Nigerian secondary school students to make smart, informed decisions about their future.

Our Story: Why We Exist

Every year, over 1.8 million Nigerian students sit for the Senior Secondary Certificate Examination (SSCE), both internal (WAEC/NECO) and external (GCE). They spend six formative years in secondary school—years that are supposed to prepare them for life, for careers, for purpose. Yet, when it’s time to choose a university course, a polytechnic programme, or a vocational path, the vast majority of these students are navigating in the dark.

Data from the Joint Admissions and Matriculation Board (JAMB) consistently shows that a huge percentage of candidates apply for courses they don’t fully understand, guided only by hearsay, peer pressure, or parental expectations. The result? High dropout rates, course changes, frustration, and a mismatch between Nigeria’s workforce needs and the skills being produced.

We built Course Guide to change that narrative.

We are not just another educational website. We are a decision‑making companion. Our mission is simple: to give every secondary school student—whether in a bustling city like Lagos or a quiet village in Ebonyi State—the tools, knowledge, and confidence to decide what comes next. Because choosing a career shouldn’t be a gamble. It should be an informed, guided, and exciting journey.

What We Believe

At Course Guide, we believe:
  • Every student has potential. No child is “average.” When matched with the right opportunities and the right information, every young Nigerian can find a path where they’ll thrive.
  • Information should be free and accessible. The difference between a student who makes a great choice and one who doesn’t is often not intelligence—it’s access. We’re closing that gap, one school, one student, one click at a time.
  • Guidance should be holistic. Academics alone aren’t enough. We consider your personality, your interests, your current grades, your preferred work style, and even your home environment to suggest realistic, personalised options.
  • Nigeria’s future depends on today’s students. With over 60% of our population under 25, the choices today’s teenagers make will shape the economy, innovation, and leadership of tomorrow’s Nigeria. We take that seriously.

Our Approach to Content

Every piece of information on Course Guide is:

  • Researched & Verified: We cross‑check official JAMB brochures, university handbooks, and government policy documents.
  • Student‑Friendly: Complex admission jargon is translated into plain English that a 14‑year‑old can understand.
  • Regularly Updated: Admission requirements change. Cut‑off marks fluctuate. New courses are accredited. We keep our database current so you’re never relying on outdated information.
  • Culturally Relevant: We root our advice in the Nigerian context—discussing our unique admission system, labour market realities, and cultural expectations—not generic, foreign career advice.

Our Vision for the Future

By 2030, we aim to:

  • Reach 5 million Nigerian secondary school students across all 36 states and the FCT.
  • Translate our core content into Hausa, Yoruba, Igbo, and Pidgin English to reach students in underserved, non‑English‑dominant homes.
  • Build an offline‑first mobile app that works even in areas with poor internet connectivity, ensuring no student is left behind.
  • Partner with government bodies, JAMB, and State Ministries of Education to integrate our guidance tools into the national curriculum and pre‑admission processes.
We dream of a Nigeria where no student finishes secondary school wondering, "What next?" without a clear, confident answer.
