Our Story: Why We Exist

Every year, over 1.8 million Nigerian students sit for the Senior Secondary Certificate Examination (SSCE), both internal (WAEC/NECO) and external (GCE). They spend six formative years in secondary school—years that are supposed to prepare them for life, for careers, for purpose. Yet, when it’s time to choose a university course, a polytechnic programme, or a vocational path, the vast majority of these students are navigating in the dark.

Data from the Joint Admissions and Matriculation Board (JAMB) consistently shows that a huge percentage of candidates apply for courses they don’t fully understand, guided only by hearsay, peer pressure, or parental expectations. The result? High dropout rates, course changes, frustration, and a mismatch between Nigeria’s workforce needs and the skills being produced.

We built Course Guide to change that narrative

We are not just another educational website. We are a decision‑making companion. Our mission is simple: to give every secondary school student—whether in a bustling city like Lagos or a quiet village in Ebonyi State—the tools, knowledge, and confidence to decide what comes next. Because choosing a career shouldn’t be a gamble. It should be an informed, guided, and exciting journey.

What We Believe

  • Personalised Career Exploration: Not everyone should be a doctor, and not everyone should be an engineer. Using our interactive assessment tools, students can discover careers that genuinely align with their interests, strengths, and personality. From Agriculture to Zoology, we cover the full spectrum of courses offered in Nigerian tertiary institutions, as well as emerging fields like Data Science, Cybersecurity, and Renewable Energy.
  • Course vs. Subject Matching: One of the biggest mistakes students make is choosing subjects in SS1 that automatically disqualify them from their dream courses later. Our platform analyses JAMB subject combination requirements and O’Level prerequisites in real time, alerting students early if their subject mix won’t open the doors they want.
  • University & Polytechnic Navigator: With over 170 universities, polytechnics, and colleges of education in Nigeria, deciding where to apply can be overwhelming. We provide up‑to‑date information on admission requirements, cut‑off marks, school fees, location, and available courses across federal, state, and private institutions—so students and parents can compare options side by side.
  • Progress Tracking & Study Tips: Secondary school is a marathon, not a sprint. Our platform lets students log their termly results, visualise their academic progress over time, and get practical, subject‑specific study advice. Whether you’re struggling with Mathematics or aiming for a distinction in Literature, we’ve got actionable tips backed by learning science.
